# Everyday Things, Two Lines

Two lines that explain one everyday thing to a 10-year-old — a fridge — with one concrete, surprising analogy, then an honest account of what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## The entry

> A fridge is like a team of movers carrying heat out of the box and dumping it behind the fridge, not making cold at all.
> It maps the heat leaving and the warm back, but it leaves out the compressor and the gas that move the heat.

Two lines, 229 characters. Line 1 explains a fridge to a 10-year-old with one analogy — a team of movers carrying heat out, not making cold. Line 2 says exactly what the analogy maps (the heat leaving, the warm back of the fridge) and what it leaves out (the compressor and the gas that do the moving).

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline:

```sh
node main.ts
```
